2. **Bracket stop prices are computed from the pre-fill signal price, not the actual average cost, so the live stop % drifts from the documented 10%.** Code confirms the mechanism: `run_rsi2_bot.py:203` and `run_ibkr_bot.py:110` both call `place_bracket_buy(..., signal.price, settings.stop_loss_pct)`, and `ibkr/orders.py:47` sets `stop_price = round(entry_price * (1 - stop_loss_pct), 2)` using that signal price ΓÇö not the market order's actual fill price. The stop audit (`protection.py:42-47`) only recomputes a stop when none exists (`"ok"` if `working_stop_qty >= position_qty`, regardless of whether that stop matches real `avg_cost`), so a stop set from a stale signal price is never corrected after the fill. Today's three open stock positions all show the drift: `V STK 5 @397.20` with `STP 352.29` (ΓêÆ11.3%, looser than 10%), `AMAT STK 4 @432.25` with `STP 401.97` (ΓêÆ7.0%, tighter), `LRCX STK 6 @275.96` with `STP 258.23` (ΓêÆ6.4%, tighter).
