## Problems found

1. **Trend Join's live-quote fetch doesn't catch NaN bid/ask, so the fallback to last-bar-close never engages and the entire exit-management loop ran blind for ~80 minutes.** `get_quote` (`src/tradingbot/options/live_chain.py:54-55`) only raises when `bid is None or ask is None or bid <= 0 or ask <= 0`; a `nan` bid/ask satisfies none of those (`nan <= 0` is `False` in Python), so it's returned as if valid. `run_trend_join_bot.py:248-251` only falls back to the last completed bar on a `ValueError`, which never fires here. Downstream, `plan_exit` (`strategy/trend_join.py:222,225`) compares `price <= pos.stop` and computes `r_mult = (price - entry) / r` ΓÇö both silently `False`/`nan` against a nan price, so the stop-hit safety net, the +0.75R partial sell, the +1R breakeven move, and the swing-low trail were all inert for the whole trade. Evidence: `2026-10-20 14:11:07,153 INFO HAS: holding 18, price nan, entry 108.40, stop 100.39, nanR` repeated ~80 times until `15:51:16,720 INFO HAS: CLOSED 18 @ 109.22 (eod_flatten)`. No loss resulted today only because price never approached the stop or the partial/breakeven thresholds; a larger intraday swing would have gone completely unmanaged except by the static broker-side stop.
2. **The journal-vs-real-fill price mismatch (continuing from 10-15/10-16) recurred in ORB and now newly appears in Trend Join too.** ORB: journaled `actual entry premium=1.38`, pnl 250.00, vs IBKR's real `SPY 20261020 768.0C | SLD | 10.00 | 1.39 | 1.63 | realized_pnl 238.21` ΓÇö an $11.79 gap (stale $0.01 entry price ├ù 10 contracts ├ù 100, plus ~$1.79 commission never subtracted). Trend Join: journaled `entry_price 108.40`, realized_pnl 14.76, vs raw `HAS | SLD | 18.00 | 108.46 | 109.22 | realized_pnl 12.72` ΓÇö a $2.04 gap from the same cause.
